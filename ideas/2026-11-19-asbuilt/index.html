      Purchase to pay

      Northwind Foods buys ambient and chilled stock from 212 active vendors across three trading regions. The purchase to pay process was rebuilt during implementation to put a single spending gate in front of the Controller, and to make freight recoverable at the line level rather than being absorbed into margin.

      Every purchase order is raised by a buyer against a vendor with an agreed posting group. Orders at or under the approval limit post straight through; anything above routes to the Controller through the approval workflow. Receiving is done against the order in the warehouse, and invoices are matched three ways — order, receipt, invoice — before they can be posted. Where a quantity or price falls outside tolerance the invoice is held and appears on the buyer's role centre, not the Controller's.

      Why it is set this way

      “Anything over 25k has to come to me. I don't care if it's pallets of tinned tomatoes — if it's over 25 I sign it.” Discovery workshop 3, 11 Feb 2026 — Controller, Northwind Foods (transcript 00:18:42)

      Behind this

      Freight surcharge lines on purchase invoices are produced by the extension NW_FreightSurcharge v1.2.0 (AppSource ID 8f3a…c71), which subscribes to the purchase line validate event and inserts a charge (item) line per shipment agent. It is not standard Business Central. If this extension is uninstalled, freight recovery stops silently.
