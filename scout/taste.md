# Taste file

Projects I admire and why they worked. The scout uses these to calibrate what "simple but cool" means. Add your own; one or two lines each is enough.

## Builds
- **roomGPT** (Hassan El Mghari, @nutlope): upload a photo of your room, get it redesigned in a chosen style. One input, one striking output; free, open source, and the result is the thing people share.
- **restorePhotos** (@nutlope): turns old, blurry face photos into clear ones. Emotional, instantly understood, and everyone has a photo to try it on.
- **LlamaCoder** (@nutlope): describe a small app and get working code with a live preview, built on an open model days after it came out. Rides a new capability while it is fresh.
- **Blinkshot** (@nutlope): images appear as fast as you type. Speed itself is the wow.
- **Napkins** (@nutlope): a screenshot or sketch of a UI becomes code. Shows an obvious before and after.
- **Research papers as a shelf of books** (seen on X): thousands of papers presented as a browsable library. Public data, one clever presentation, useful every day.

What these share: one input box, a result in seconds, no signup, open source with the stack listed, and a launch post that is mostly the demo.
